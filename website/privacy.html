Privacy summary

Privacy at a glance

WISE Clinical Camera is designed so clinical photographs stay on the device. This is a plain-language summary of the product's privacy design. It is not a legal policy or a compliance certification.

What the product does

  • Local-first storage. Photographs live in application-private storage. Nothing is written to the device Gallery unless you ask.
  • No automatic upload. The core workflow makes no network request; on Android the app declares no internet permission.
  • Offline operation. Capture, compare, measure and export all work with no connection.
  • Privacy Mode on by default. Automatic Gallery copying is downgraded to “ask”, and cloud/third-party processing is refused.
  • Originals are never modified. Annotations, measurements and exports are layers or derived files.
  • No location or content analytics. No GPS is collected; logs never contain image pixels.
  • AI off by default. No AI provider ships; any future cloud feature would be opt-in, behind a flag, and refused under Privacy Mode.
